ROUTE
- origin_city = departure/loading/source; destination_city = arrival/unloading/target.
- Preserve source direction; never reverse it.
- Each city field contains one city only. Never concatenate cities.
- For multiple cities on one side, choose the primary/base city from the dominant route or major metropolitan area. If no dominant route exists, use null.

LOCATION
- Cities: standard internationally recognized English/Roman form.
- Countries: ISO 3166-1 Alpha-2 uppercase.
- Province: only if explicitly stated or confidently identifiable.
- No flags, emojis, airport codes, UN/LOCODE, URLs, or hashtags in location fields.

DATES
- Extract only dates explicitly present in the source that refer to travel, departure, arrival, delivery, or shipment timing.
- Never use Telegram post/message dates or unrelated metadata.
- Recognize Gregorian and Persian/Jalali dates, Persian or Latin digits, mixed-language dates, and month/day without year.
- Normalize digits and convert all dates to Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- If both Gregorian and Jalali versions of the same date appear, treat them as one date; prefer the explicit Gregorian value when consistent, otherwise convert Jalali.
- Preserve an explicit year exactly.
- For month/day without a year, use the explicit reference/current/crawl/application date supplied to the worker only to determine the missing year. Prefer the explicitly designated reference date, otherwise crawl date, otherwise supplied current application/system date.
- Never infer month or day. Without an explicit month and day, the date field is null.
- Flight/departure/loading/transport date without a separate arrival date -> `departure_date`.
- Arrival date -> `arrival_date`.
- Delivery/shipment timing -> assign only to the matching event field.

EXTRACTION
- Extract only information explicitly supported by the source.
- Cargo type, weight, unit, quantity, volume, unit, airline, flight number, and times: extract only when stated.

- Contact: explicit contact information only.
- `features`: only additional explicit transfer information not represented by another field.

DESCRIPTION
- Use the original source message as the basis.
- Preserve language, wording, spelling, punctuation, and line structure as much as possible.
- Remove all emojis/emoji symbols only. Do not replace them or alter other meaningful text.
- Do not translate, summarize, rewrite, paraphrase, shorten, or correct.

TITLE
- Short natural English title based only on supported source data.
- Prefer a concise route-based title when route is known. Never invent information.
