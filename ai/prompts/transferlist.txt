## ROUTE

`destination_city` = arrival/unloading/target city.

Preserve the source direction. Never reverse it.

Each field must contain exactly ONE city.

## DATES

Extract every explicit date that represents travel, departure, arrival, delivery, or shipment timing.

A date is valid evidence only when the date itself appears in the source message. Never use a Telegram message/post date, database timestamp, crawl date, processing date, current date, or any other metadata as the travel/shipment date unless that date is explicitly present in the source message text.

A date is valid evidence even when it is embedded inside a sentence, placed next to emojis, separated by punctuation, or written in a nonstandard textual form.

Recognize date expressions in all relevant forms, including:

* Gregorian dates written with digits or month names
* Persian/Jalali dates written with Persian digits or Latin digits
* dates written partly in Persian and partly in English
* dates containing a Gregorian date and a Persian/Jalali equivalent
* month-and-day expressions without an explicit year
* dates with surrounding words such as "on", "in", "at", "date", "تاریخ", "در", "روز", or similar date markers

Normalize Persian and Gregorian digits before interpreting the date.

If both Gregorian and Persian/Jalali representations of the same date are present, treat them as two representations of ONE date, not two separate dates. Prefer the explicitly stated Gregorian date when both are consistent; otherwise convert the Persian/Jalali date to Gregorian and use the consistent result.

Convert all extracted dates to Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`.

If the year is explicitly stated in the source text, preserve that year.

### STRICT YEAR RULE

A month-and-day expression without an explicit year, such as `9 August`, `9 آگوست`, `9 Aug`, or `۹ آگوست`, MUST NOT be assigned a year by guessing.

Do NOT copy the year from:
- the Telegram message/post date
- the crawl date
- the processing date
- the current application date
- another unrelated date in metadata
- an unrelated date elsewhere in the message

If a date has no explicit year and the source text does not contain a clear, explicit year that belongs to that same date, return `null` for that date field.

Example: `تاریخ پرواز: 9 آگوست` means the flight date is explicitly `9 آگوست`, but because no year is stated, `departure_date` MUST be `null`. Never turn it into `2023-08-09`, `2024-08-09`, `2025-08-09`, `2026-08-09`, or any other year.

If the same date is explicitly written with a year elsewhere in the source, use that year only when the text clearly indicates that both expressions refer to the same event/date.

When a date is explicitly connected to a flight, departure, loading, or transport route and no separate arrival date is given, assign it to `departure_date`.

When a date is explicitly connected to arrival, assign it to `arrival_date`.

When a date is explicitly connected to delivery/shipment timing, preserve the distinction between shipment timing and travel timing and assign it only to the field that matches the stated event.

Never lose an explicitly year-qualified date merely because it is written in Persian, Jalali, mixed-language text, or informal formatting.

Never invent dates or times.
